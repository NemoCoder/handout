$$2^{\mu}(\sqrt{x})^{\lambda+\mu}J_{\lambda+\mu}(\sqrt{x})=\frac{1}{\Gamma(\mu)}\int_{0}^{x}(x-y)^{\mu-1}(\sqrt{y})^{\lambda}J_{\lambda}(\sqrt{y})\,dy.$$
