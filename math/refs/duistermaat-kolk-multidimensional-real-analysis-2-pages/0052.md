(a) The function $g:R\rightarrow R$ defined by
$g(x)=0\qquad(x\leq 0);\qquad g(x)=e^{-1/x}\qquad(x>0)$

is a $C^{\infty}$ function; this is also true at $x=0$ , where all derivatives vanish.
