$$ \begin{align*}\pi f(y)&=\frac{d}{dy}\int_{0}^{1}\frac{\sqrt{y}\,g(yt)}{\sqrt{1-t}}\,dt=\int_{0}^{1}\frac{\frac{1}{2\sqrt{y}}\,g(yt)+t\sqrt{y}\,g^{\prime}(yt)}{\sqrt{1-t}}\,dt\\ &=\int_{0}^{y}\frac{g(x)+2x g^{\prime}(x)}{2y\sqrt{y-x}}\,dx.\end{align*} $$
