Since f is real-valued, Ψ(f) is self-adjoint (Theorem 13.24), and since f(λ)(1-λ)=i(1+λ), the multiplication theorem gives
(5) Ψ(f)(I-U)=i(I+U).

(7) (Ax,y)=∫ΩfdE′x,y [x∈D(A),y∈H].
