(6) $ \|h_{\lambda}\|_{1} \leq \int_{R^{n}} |f(y)| \, dm_{n}(y) \int_{R^{n}} |g(\xi) - g(\xi - \lambda^{-1}y)| \, dm_{n}(\xi) $,
