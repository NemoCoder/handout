Thus if $z\in D$ then $$ m(z)=m_{\alpha}m_{\alpha}^{-1}m(z)=m_{\alpha}(e^{i\theta}z)=\frac{e^{i\theta}z+\alpha}{\bar{\alpha}e^{i\theta}z+1}. $$

Note also that
