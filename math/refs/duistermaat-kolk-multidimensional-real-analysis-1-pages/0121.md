$$ p(x)=p(x)-p(c)=\sum\limits_{0\leq i\leq n}a_{i}(x^{i}-c^{i})=(x-c)\,q(x)\qquad(x\in R). $$

$$ p^{\prime}(x)=q(x)+(x-c)q^{\prime}(x) $$
