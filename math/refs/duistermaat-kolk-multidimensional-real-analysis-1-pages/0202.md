π² / sin²(πx) = Σ_{k ∈ Z} 1/(x - k)²,

(iv) Prove by (2n - 2)-fold differentiation
π²n / (2n - 1)! = 2²n Σ_{k ∈ Z} 1/(2x - 2k - 1)² (n ∈ N, x - 1/2 ∈ R \ Z).

Here we have defined ζ(2n) = Σ_{k ∈ N} 1/k²n. Conclude that ζ(2) = π²/6 and
