$$x_{k}=x_{0}\frac{1-r_{0}^{2^{k}}}{1-r_{0}}=x_{0}\sum_{0\leq j<2^{k}}r_{0}^{j}\qquad(k\in N_{0}),$$ 

 which amounts to summing the first 2k terms of the geometric series

$$\frac{1}{y}=\frac{x_{0}}{1-r_{0}}=x_{0}\sum_{j\in N_{0}}r_{0}^{j}.$$
